SysFEAT provides a unifying ontological foundation for conceptual modeling and enterprise architecture by:

● Bridging ontology with mathematical formalization by grounding its constructs in the Agda proof assistant.
● Extending established modeling standards—ISO 15926 , BORO, UFO, KerML, and SBVR—with lexical scoping, compositionality, and modularity as first-class principles for handling locality, context, and multi-level abstraction.
● Capturing temporal and extensional identity through a 4D semantic grounding built on KerML, ISO 15926, BORO, and Matthew West’s work.
● Incorporating Russell Ackoff’s formalization of human behavior to better represent agent-driven dynamics and socially constructed systems.
● Integrating enterprise economics principles into enterprise architecting, aligning architectural decisions with economic rationale.

In doing so, SysFEAT provides a unifying meta-ontological foundation that harmonizes and extends existing standards to better model, reason about, and manage the 
complexity of socio-technical and enterprise systems
